One loan. Repaid by your refund.

The Accelerator Loan is for biotech and medtech companies eligible for the R&D Tax Incentive and running preclinical or clinical trials in Australia. Draw on your annual refund now, need less capital upfront, and delay your next equity raise.

  1. ⚗
    You spend on eligible R&DPreclinical or clinical work carried out in Australia.
  2. ✦
    We advance part of your expected refundFunds released the same day you sign.
  3. ◇
    You lodge your return as usualWith your R&D tax advisor and AusIndustry registration.
  4. ◎
    Your ATO refund repays the loanLoan amount and interest are deducted from the refund.

From application to funding.

Most of it is paperwork you already have.

  1. Apply online

    A short form that takes about a minute.

  2. Get a call back

    We call within 24 hours and explain the documents we need.

  3. Send your documents

    Comfort letter from your R&D tax advisor, AusIndustry registration and last year’s tax return.

  4. Review and sign

    Loan documents follow approval.

  5. Funded the same day

    Funds are released on signing when documents arrive before 2pm AEST.
